Audio

What listeners see

RDS sends text alongside the audio. Radios that support it (most car radios) show it on their display. It only goes out while RDS is on and the stereo pilot is locked (see Status).
The short name every RDS radio shows, up to 8 characters. Set it once and leave it: radios expect it to stay fixed, and changing it every few seconds distracts drivers.
A longer message, up to 64 characters, that radios show on request or scroll. Good for "Now playing" or your phone number. Playlist software can update it automatically (see Settings › API). Accents are converted to plain letters.

Station identity

Turns the whole RDS data signal on or off. Off means no station name or text on radios.
Program Identification: a 4-character hex code (0–9, A–F) that radios use to tell stations apart and to remember presets. For a low-power station, pick any code no nearby station uses, then don't change it.
Your station's format. Some radios show it or let listeners search by it. Uses the North American (RBDS) list.

Front-panel display

The FM30's display shows two lines of 8 characters. It steps through these pages, about 3 seconds each:
Pages 6–8 are yours. Use them for a station slogan, a request-line number, or what's on next. Empty pages are skipped.

Your messages

Each message has a top line (up to 8 characters, stays still) and a bottom line (up to 48 characters; anything longer than 8 scrolls). Leave the top line empty to use only the bottom line. The preview shows what the display will look like.

Tuning internals

🔒 Locked. to change these.
For bring-up and calibration only. Leave VTSCALE at 3.0 unless a meter on C38 says otherwise.

RDS tuning

How strongly the RDS signal is mixed in (needs the control password). Aim for 3–5 % of the signal, about 2–4 kHz of deviation, checked with an RDS analyzer or SDR. Too low and radios drop RDS; too high adds noise to the audio.
Fine-tunes the RDS signal's timing against the 19 kHz stereo pilot, in degrees. Adjust while watching the error rate on an RDS analyzer; otherwise leave it at 0.
Normally RDS waits for the stereo pilot so it stays in step with it. On = send RDS even without a pilot (mono, or no audio input). Use for testing.
TP (traffic program): this station carries traffic reports. TA (traffic announcement): a report is on air right now; radios may cut over to it, so leave it off. MS: on = music, off = speech. Stereo: tells radios the audio is stereo.
